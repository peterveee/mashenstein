## Write your own: a brief mention

Not a big part of the video: one beat to say you can write your own, and a little of the dance floor.

- **Write your own**: draw a riff, pick a style and a mood, and THE LAB makes a whole song. The birth scene is the quickest way to show it: Gary throws the switch, the cassette comes alive, IT'S ALIVE!
- **A little of the dance floor**: one or two hero moves, such as Lorenzo taking the floor underwater with the paper fish, or B-33P switching the room to 8-bit with the CRT. The flying toaster or the BOLT's new take if there is room.
