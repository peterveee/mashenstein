## Features

Speed Zone
- **Coyote winking and howling**
- **Coyote chorus**: a pup yips, then howls with the parent
- **New terrain** (the MCM act)

Frost
- **New sky** (crayon)
- **Santa's sleigh flypast**: Rudolph's blinking nose, Santa waving, stardust
- **Arctic fox** diving head-first into the snow
- **Three wolves** on a ledge howling in turn (pairs with the coyote)

Crypt
- **All-new scenery** (gouache)
- **Gate slamming** (the one slam per stage, on the beat)
- **Clouds turning dark** (the eclipse: cloud train rolls over the moon, lane dims, gas lamps flicker on)
- **Balloon clown** lets his balloon go and watches it float off (crypt-2)
- **Witch and her cat** on a broomstick, and her glide across the moon near the finish
- **Graveyard critters**: black cat's arch and stretch, owl swivelling its head, crow flying a loop
- **Sheet ghosts** rising from graves behind the crest, 1–3 at a time

Plumber Panic
- **The sheep-pig**: a Babe piglet herding sheep in a figure of eight while the collie watches
- **Pixel to paper**: plumber-1 opens in 8-bit on the CRT (the hero has just jumped into the cabinet), then the tube powers down on bar 4 beat 4 and the stage lands as paper

Gravity Grid
- **New theme, TIME VORTEX**: 70s sci-fi TV, a Radiophonic gallop and theremin glides
- **Ten new obstacles**: foil crates, specimen cases, fuel cells, maintenance bots, coolant radiators, valve manifolds, welding drones, loose toolkits, plasma vents, mag clamps; and the levels are busier
- **Live wires**: the spike beds on the wrong rail are now crackling tesla wires
- **The finish**: the saucer shouts ACK! ACK! at the end of gravity-3, and the lone Martian waves after the shot
- **Little things**: the Earth has an orbiting moon; a flickering GRAVITY READINGS sign (18% on level 3)

Corporate Kombat
- **All-new look** (Ligne '89): the three stages are three floors of one tower, the lobby at 9am, floor 23 at noon and floor 88 at dusk, with the view out of the window showing the climb
- **Each floor has its own obstacles**: wet-floor signs, mop buckets, velvet ropes and a paper plane in the lobby; shredders, birthday bunting and party balloons on 23; founder busts, drinks trolleys, chandeliers and champagne corks on 88 (paper plane not yet seen)
- **The lifts**: EGGCORP posters in the cars, and a Martian riding the lobby lift once you've beaten gravity-3
- **Coffee shop**: a barista frothing milk, and a priced menu that ends EGG MARKET PRICE
- **80s diner booths**, and the canteen notice: TODAY: EGG AGAIN
- **Window cleaners** on floor 23, one of them hanging next to a HANG IN THERE poster
- **New song**: CORPORATE KOMBAT, opening on four bars of lobby muzak

Déjà View (the Surge)
- **The cabinet glitching out**: the backdrop changes every 4 bars by a random move (power down, tape rewind, block shatter, zoom through), and the glitching gets worse from surge-1 to surge-3 until it reaches the lane
- **Upside down**: surge-3 flips the whole screen for a bar or two
- **The HUD in the wrong place**: the top of the HUD slides down the screen, melts, drops and bounces, shreds, or swaps corners

Results
- **8-bit results**: the level's song switches to 8-bit sounds on the results screen (not yet heard)

Jukebox
- **FISH TANK visualiser**: cut-paper fish schooling, a sideways crab, the tide rising and falling on the beat
- **Plays on the lock screen** (iPhone), with cover art on the NOW PLAYING card
- **REPEAT** switch

The food court
- **Somewhere to sit**: bolted plastic tables in the plaza, under the postcard mural (a skyline of fries, a drink, a burger, a pretzel and a pizza)
- **Food stalls**: the cabinets now stand in threes with a stall in each gap, PRETZEL PALACE open and WOK THIS WAY shuttered
- **REPAIRS** and **PAWN SHOP** signs over Dolores's counters

Everywhere
- **Low gravity buys airtime**: your jumps hang in the air, and the ground stays in shot (not yet seen on a device)

## Write your own: a brief mention

Not a big part of the video: one beat to say you can write your own, and a little of the dance floor.

- **Write your own**: draw a riff, pick a style and a mood, and THE LAB makes a whole song. The birth scene is the quickest way to show it: Gary throws the switch, the cassette comes alive, IT'S ALIVE!
- **A little of the dance floor**: one or two hero moves, such as Lorenzo taking the floor underwater with the paper fish, or B-33P switching the room to 8-bit with the CRT. The flying toaster or the BOLT's new take if there is room.
- **Tunes you know on ZAP**: nursery rhymes, carols and classics, among them the CAN-CAN, FÜR ELISE and the Lemmings tunes; hold ZAP to CHOOSE A TUNE (most not yet heard)
