# MASHENSTEIN: THE UNPLUGGENING — THE SCRIPT

*The production script, kept in lockstep with the game. Every quoted line is verbatim from source.*

**Voice rule, stated in [jokes.js](src/data/jokes.js):** *absolute deadpan sincerity. Jokes never replace usable information.* Everything is uppercase in-game.

**Cast:** eight heroes + Eggshell + Gary + Dolores + the Dust Devil (plus Miss Chomp, retired from the relay but still in the building's lore).
**Runtime shape:** 10-shot, 33.13-second opening film → 3 acts / 9 cabinets / 27 stages (each opening on a briefing screen) → 3 bosses → 9 finale beats, then a coda and a curtain call.

**DON K. EGGSHELL, PHD** — *"A GRIEVANCE INTENSIFIES."* Antagonist. A giant egg-shaped ape with a magnificent red mustache, tiny science goggles, and a spiky shell. Lost to heroes for 40 straight fiscal years. Never appears as a fair fight; appears as a grievance.

**GARY, STILL ON THE CLOCK** — *"TECHNICALLY I NEVER CLOCKED OUT."* Deceased pawn-shop clerk. No hero entry; exists as an NPC, a shop, and an avatar — and as the only entity in the room with real hands. Resolves the plot by accident. Works his counter at the service end of the concourse. HR has not approved the leave his death would normally be.

**DOLORES, NOT YET RELIEVED** — *"NEXT."* Food court counter staff, still on shift. No hero entry; exists as an NPC and as the serving line itself — DOLORES' REPAIR COUNTER is her steam table, and repairs are priced and portioned like lunch. She does not acknowledge that the arcade is dead, because as far as her shift is concerned it is not over. She calls NEXT to an empty concourse; the menu board above her still reads NOW SERVING 0.

**DUST DEVIL 9000** — *"DEEP CLEAN ENGAGED."* A haunted vacuum. Cleans something impossible in the background of every act. Becomes a boss — at the end of Act I, not Act II (see the Act I boss section below). Prints a certificate.

**MISS CHOMP, IN RESIDENCE** — *"THE FOOD COURT IS MY HOMELAND. I AM ITS QUEEN."* Retired from the relay, not from the arcade: Kiko took her double-jump slot in the eight and Miss Chomp went back to the food court she always claimed as home. Currently nowhere in the building and left out of the hub roll call — a cameo at most, never a precedent for bringing her back.

*First time you open any cabinet, a breaker-box minigame runs.*

> BREAKER BOX: *(one of)* BLOCK SURGE · PADDLE WAR · MASH INVADERS · BRICK BONK

- **BLOCK SURGE** — falling five-cell pieces, deliberately not the classic seven. Marginal note: `THESE ARE NOT THE / SHAPES YOU KNOW. / LEGALLY.`

**The mission redesign (17 Oct 2026).** Every stage's mission is now simply to reach the finish line before its clock runs out. What used to be each stage's objective — the letters, the crates, the copter, the safes — is now that stage's one optional **bonus challenge**, shown below per stage; clearing it is still worth a plug, same as before. Escort and rescue goals have been retired entirely. The clock itself (three tiers — 100s / 150s / 200s by stage length) is the new way a run can fail: replayed ground, not just a depleted battery, now costs you the stage.

# THE FINALE

*Nine beats. Typewriter, 34 chars/sec. Triggered by clearing DÉJÀ VIEW, or by walking into THE SOCKET in the hub.*

**1.** *(Eggshell)*
> THE HEROES REACH THE SOCKET.

**2.** *(Eggshell)*
> EGGSHELL BLOCKS IT WITH HIS ENTIRE BODY. HE BEGINS HIS ULTIMATE MONOLOGUE. IT AUTOSCROLLS.

**3.** *(Eggshell)*
> THE HEROES PLUG THE EXTENSION CORD INTO HIS CLOWN-COPTER.

**4.** *(Eggshell)*
> NOTHING HAPPENS. THE WALL SWITCH IS OFF.

**5.** *(Eggshell)*
> GARY CASUALLY FLIPS THE SWITCH. HR WILL CITE HIM FOR UNAUTHORIZED INITIATIVE.

**6.** *(Dust Devil)*
> EGGSHELL, WARMED BY WALL-SOCKET ELECTRICITY: "SO THIS IS THE WARMTH I NEVER GOT."

**7.**
> DUST DEVIL 9000 PRINTS AN EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH CERTIFICATE FROM SOMEWHERE IT SHOULD NOT CONTAIN A PRINTER.

**8.** *(overlaid: `OVERTIME UNLOCKED`)*
> THE POWER STRIP WAS PLUGGED INTO ITSELF THE ENTIRE TIME. NOBODY ADDRESSES THIS.

**9.**
> THE LIGHTS GO OFF. THE POWER STRIP DOES NOT.

> **The shape of the ending.** The heroes do not win the fight — they complete a *task*, and the task turns out to have been mis-specified. The villain is defeated by warmth, the plot is resolved by an unauthorized employee, the central object was self-referential the whole time, and the game signs off on a quiet, unresolved image rather than a punchline. Every beat is an institutional failure played as a happy ending.

**Then a pause, and the coda** — drawn small, muted and late, like a disclaimer because it is one:
> HR HAS APPROVED NOTHING THAT HAPPENS FROM HERE ON.

**Then the curtain call.** Eight heroes, one row, all cheering at once — the only time the cast stands together on a story screen, because the campaign is one hero at a time by budget and has never let them before:

> THANK YOU FOR PLAYING
> THE ENTIRE CAST THANKS YOU FOR YOUR PATRONAGE. YOU ARE WELCOME HERE ANY NIGHT.
> HR APPROVED THE GRATITUDE. ONLY THE GRATITUDE.

**After the finale:** THE SOCKET in the hub is replaced by the **OVERTIME CABINET**. Its mission text is:

> RUN. FOREVER. THAT IS THE WHOLE DEAL.

Cleared cabinets also unlock **corrupted modifiers** — the game degrading on purpose:

| | |
|---|---|
| MAXIMUM SPEED | EVERYTHING IS FASTER. NOTHING IS CALMER. |
| RANDOM TAGS | PORTALS ARRIVE TWICE AS OFTEN. NOBODY ASKED. |

## THE CHORUS — DEATH AND JUDGEMENT

*Because you will see these more than anything else in the game, they carry more tone than the cutscenes do. This whole system was split apart since the previous draft of this script: a death used to draw from one shared pool, and now the game picks from the ARCADE's general commentary, or — if a specific thing killed you — from that thing's own voice.*

**The arcade's general commentary** (`FAIL_MESSAGES` — any death, regardless of cause):
> DEFEATED BY GEOMETRY
> TOO HEROIC FOR CURRENT RAM
> GRAVITY REMAINS UNDEFEATED
> UNPLUGGED FOR SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE
> THE FLOOR FILED A COMPLAINT
> RUNNING WAS THE EASY PART
> THE ARCADE REGRETS THIS OUTCOME

**A barrel's own voice** (killed by a barrel or fire barrel specifically):
> A BARREL HAS WON THE ARGUMENT

**Falling in a hole gets its own pool** (`PIT_FAIL_MESSAGES`), deadpan rather than a taunt — the player already knows they missed the jump:
> GRAVITY REMAINS UNDEFEATED
> THE FLOOR WAS NOT THERE. IT HAD NEVER BEEN THERE.
> DOWN IS THE ONE DIRECTION THAT ALWAYS WORKS
> A HOLE. WORKING EXACTLY AS INTENDED.
> PLUMBING FAILURE. YOURS.
> THAT WAS A GAP. IT REMAINS A GAP.
> NO NOTES. TEXTBOOK DESCENT.

**And what was at the bottom, said out loud** (`FILL_FAIL_MESSAGES`, keyed by the pit's fill material):
> **Tar:** THE TAR ACCEPTS ALL APPLICANTS / THE TAR IS NOT A FLOOR. IT WAS NEVER A FLOOR.
> **Lava:** THE FLOOR WAS WARMER THAN ADVERTISED / MOLTEN. BRIEFLY YOURS.
> **Slush:** THE WATER WAS COLD AND UNIMPRESSED / BLACK ICE. NO NOTES.

> **Gears:** THE GEARBOX ACCEPTS ALL DEPOSITS / MAINTENANCE WILL NOTE THE OBSTRUCTION / THE MACHINE DID NOT NOTICE YOU

## APPENDIX B — WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE PREVIOUS DRAFT

1. **RUSTY replaced GNASH in the eight-hero roster (10 Sep 2026).** Same speedster slot, same stats; his tagline, joke, hub lines and exit lines are all still Gnash's, on loan, pending his own writing. Gnash is held in the sprite data for a possible NPC return.

2. **LORENZO'S ABILITY CHANGED (10 Sep 2026).** The old air-stomp/ground-smash STOMP/SMASH was replaced by the PIPE WRENCH: a thrown, returning weapon that uses the same out-hover-home flight as Grumpos' axe. His mastery sidegrade changed to match — SECOND BITE, not the old SHOCK STOMP.

3. **RAY M'N WAS RENAMED RAMON.** Same tagline, same joke, same Rocket Fist kit.

4. **THE AUTHORED PORTAL HAND-OFFS WERE RETIRED.** The eight two-hander exchanges between specific hero pairs no longer exist; every hero now speaks a stand-alone exit line instead (see THE RECURRING BIT — THE RELAY, above).

5. **THE MISSION SYSTEM WAS REBUILT (17 Oct 2026).** Every stage's mission is now the finish line, full stop; what used to be the mission is now the one optional bonus challenge, and a countdown clock is the new way a run fails mid-stage.

6. **CARDBOARD KINGDOM WAS REPLACED OUTRIGHT BY GRAVITY GRID (12–13 Oct 2026).** Different setting (a zero-gravity space station, not a collapsing cardboard castle), different mechanic (polarity-flip running surfaces, a hover-vacuum, low gravity), same slot in Act III and the same unlock cost.

7. **THE SURGE WAS RENAMED DÉJÀ VIEW.** Same cabinet, same remix-everything mechanic, same music bank and taunts — the display name changed, the internals did not.

8. **RHYTHM BANKRUPTCY AND TERMINAL VELOCITY SWAPPED ACT-ENDING DUTY.** Rhythm Bankruptcy closes Act I now (Dust Devil 9000 boss); Terminal Velocity closes Act II (Clown-Copter boss). The previous draft of this script had the two bosses on the wrong acts.

9. **GARY AND DOLORES' HUB CARDS WERE RE-LABELED.** "GARY (DECEASED)" is now "GARY, STILL ON THE CLOCK"; "DOLORES (ON BREAK)" is now "DOLORES, NOT YET RELIEVED." Both characters, jokes and hub lines are otherwise unchanged.

10. **THE FINALE'S LAST BEAT CHANGED**, and two new screens were added after it: an HR coda (`HR HAS APPROVED NOTHING THAT HAPPENS FROM HERE ON.`) and a full curtain-call thank-you screen.

11. **THE DEATH-MESSAGE SYSTEM WAS SPLIT.** What used to be one shared pool of "cause of death" and "results screen" lines is now four: a generic arcade-commentary pool, a barrel-specific line, a pit-specific pool, and pools keyed to what was waiting at the bottom of the pit (tar, lava, slush, spikes, gears).

**Still open:** Miss Chomp has no current placement in the building; her hub lines and NPC card are written and held in the game's data against the day she gets one.
