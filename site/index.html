MASHENSTEIN: THE UNPLUGGENING

The arcade went dark at 11:58 PM.
Who is fixing it?

WHAT HAPPENED

Every cabinet was dreaming its little electric dream. Then the lights went out in one clean tug, and they have not come back on.

Don K. Eggshell, PhD — an egg-headed professor with a wild silver mane, a pair of very small spectacles and forty consecutive fiscal years of losing to heroes — has stopped losing. He walked to the wall and pulled out the master power strip.

“IF I CANNOT WIN… NOBODY PLAYS.”

Nine cabinets are dying. There is exactly one working socket at the far end of the building. Eight heroes are still inside with him, and all eight would like a word.

Unfortunately, due to budget cuts, a cabinet can only render one hero at a time.

So it is a relay. Run, jump, and do not stop. Reach a portal and keep going as whoever comes out the other side.

There is no art on disk and no audio on disk: every character, prop, background, hazard, song and sound effect is drawn or synthesized from code while you play.

NINE CABINETS

Three acts, nine machines, nine completely different-looking games — because the arcade could not afford one art style either.

MANAGEMENT & REMAINING STAFF

The food court never closed. Nobody told it.

THE MANAGEMENT

DON K. EGGSHELL, PhD

“A GRIEVANCE INTENSIFIES.”

An egg-headed professor with a wild silver mane, a collar he can hide behind and a doctorate in statistics that has never once helped. Forty consecutive fiscal years of losing to heroes. Disputes damage by filing forms. Commutes by clown copter, which he would like on record was a deliberate aesthetic choice.

PAWN COUNTER

GARY (DECEASED)

“PHYSICAL JURISDICTION RETAINED.”

A deceased pawn-shop clerk with a detachable head, still working the counter. The only entity left in the building with real hands. That turns out to matter more than anyone expected.

REPAIR COUNTER

DOLORES

“NEXT.”

Still on shift. Portions out repairs off a steam table that used to do nachos. She has not been relieved, the lunch rush is at noon, and the board above her has read NOW SERVING 0 for some years now.

FACILITIES

DUST DEVIL 9000

“DEEP CLEAN ENGAGED.”

A haunted vacuum cleaner. Cleans the floor, then the ceiling, then the inside of a CRT. Becomes a boss fight somewhere around the ceiling. It is the only antagonist in the game with a conscience — it apologises every time it hits you.

THE FINE PRINT

WHEN DOES IT COME OUT?
Soon. A form has been filed disputing that answer.
WHAT DO I NEED TO PLAY IT?
A browser, and the ability to tap a screen or press a button. It runs in the tab you already have open, on a laptop, a phone or a tablet.
WHO DID THE MUSIC?
The game does, every time. Nine cabinets, nine soundtracks, zero audio files — it is all played live by synthesizers with names like MRDR-3, KLNG-8 and TNGR-2, which were also built for this.
HAVEN'T I SEEN THESE HEROES BEFORE?
No. You are thinking of somebody else. Every one of them was drawn and written from scratch for this game. Gary has asked it be put on record that he has never met the entities you are thinking of, that any resemblance is coincidental, and that he considers the matter closed.
CAN I PLAY AS DON K. EGGSHELL, PhD?
He has asked. Repeatedly. In writing.
HOW LONG IS IT?
Twenty-seven stages, three boss fights and one wall socket. Longer if you keep losing to the haunted vacuum cleaner, which is not a criticism — it is genuinely quite good.
