    Advanced audio

    These are Mixer-only safety controls. They protect different parts of the playback path and do not change the song, export, synthesis, or game audio.

    Output stage. This requests how much audio the browser keeps ready for the output callback. Larger buffers can absorb short output spikes, but add real key-to-sound latency and need a reload. The browser may ignore or approximate the request; the reported value below is what it actually exposes.

    Scheduling stage. This queues notes further ahead so a busy browser or heavy panel has more time to recover. Larger values make sequencer edits, loops, and mutes take longer to be heard. It applies immediately; computer-keyboard and MIDI preview remain on their separate immediate path.

    How they work together. The Audio buffer protects delivery from the audio thread to the speakers; read-ahead protects the main-thread scheduler that feeds it. They are independent, so you can increase one without changing the other. For a busy song, try Smooth playback with 500 ms or 1 second read-ahead before using Maximum safety.

    Loop diagnostics

    While the transport plays, the Mixer records each heard loop plus immediate rows for playback interruptions, complete output loss, repair attempts, recovery, and freeze exports. Profile Track Load stops playback and measures every visible track over the song’s densest eight bars, both with and without that track’s inserts. Rows persist across reloads. No console work is needed.
