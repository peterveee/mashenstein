What the Banger Sound Palette does

It sets which presets a new Banger can use for its chords, bass, leads and arpeggios. Save your changes, then reload the game before creating or recharging a Banger. Existing saved Bangers keep their current sounds until you recharge them.

1. Choose where it appliesStyle affects every mood for that style. Mood affects that mood across styles. Style + Mood affects only that pair. The preview selectors let you hear the context.

2. Choose a part and its presetsUse Chords, Bass, Leads or Arp, then choose a Musical part. Checked presets are available to the generator. Add a preset to include it; turn off a preset to remove it from the choices.

3. Compare and shape the soundHear this preset plays that exact sound with the other parts, starting just before it first enters. The teal dot pulses on this part's notes while the preview plays. After a preview, the green Used in last preview row shows which preset was heard. Adjust its Relative level for the next preview; Favourite makes it three times as likely to be picked; Effects adds channel effects.

4. Save and use it in the gameSave Changes writes the palette settings. Reload the game before generating or recharging a Banger to use them.
