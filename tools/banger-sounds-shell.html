Parts

One sound for each part the generator writes. Each dropdown offers only what the rules allow in that slot; the rest are listed, shut, with the reason. ▶ plays the sound in its real job.

Drum Kits

The kits the Kit switch picks between. Wherever a kit leaves a drum out, the Style Kit's sound plays.

Random Shortlists

What Riff Sound = Random may give the riff's own parts, by the job each part does. Random only ever picks from these lists — less the never-use list and the mood's skips.

Part Shortlists

What Part Sounds = Roll may give the parts the generator writes. Each take draws one — the style's own sound is always in the draw. A part with no list always plays the style's own, so leave a style's signature parts empty. A mood that swaps a part's sound overrides the roll.

Moods

A mood can swap any part's sound (Dark → a Reese bass), and take sounds out of the Random lists. Anything a mood does not override is the style's own choice.

Never Use

Presets no banger in this style may use — not as a part, not in a kit, not at random.

From Your Songs

Every preset the songs on disk use, and how many songs use it — the remixes first. A sound that has already worked is one Use As… away from a slot or a shortlist.
