HOW THE AUDIO REPORTS WORK

The two lines

Every song that ships is held to a loudness line, so a sound effect lands the same on every stage and gameplay is the loudest music in the game.

−21 LUFS
the nine cabinets, the finale, the credits megamix
−24 LUFS
the title, the Food Court, the shop: between-games music, 3 dB calmer

LUFS is loudness as the ear hears it, not raw energy: a sub-heavy song can carry lots of energy and still sound quiet. "On the line" means within ±0.3 dB.

After you change or remix a song

  1. Save it in the mixer.
  2. SONG LEVELS: APPLY, and wait for it to finish.
  3. Reload the song in the mixer before saving it again. The mixer writes the whole file, so saving a stale copy puts the old level back.

Good to know

  • Results are on the reports page. It updates when a run finishes, including runs from a terminal (node tools/song-levels.js, node tools/bass-report.js).
  • Every run is niced, so mixer playback comes first, but the machine will be busy while it renders.
  • Cardboard, Office and Surge are levelled but stay out of the bass median until their songs are finished.
